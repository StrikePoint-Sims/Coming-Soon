What we are looking to build

Your new home sim.

Golf season never ends. That was the idea: the feeling of having a home simulator, brought to a space you’d want to share with friends and family.

We’re exploring how to bring that vision to life in the Colchester area. Here’s what inspired the original concept.

01 / THE SPACE

Room for your game.

Spacious, private simulator bays. No crowds, no distractions, and no one standing over your shoulder. Like swinging at home.

02 / THE PRACTICE

More than a screen.

Trackman-powered golf: useful shot data for working on your swing, and virtual courses for simply enjoying a round.

03 / THE FREEDOM

Golf around real life.

Before work, after bedtime, or through a long Connecticut winter. A convenient way to keep playing when getting to the course is a challenge.
